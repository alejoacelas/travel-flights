# Preferences

These are the standing choices automation may make without asking.

- Seat: accept any free assigned seat; do not choose or change seats.
- Paid seat: never pay.
- Checked bags: none; never add or pay for one.
- Voluntary bump or travel credit: never accept.
- Schedule change: email every change; do not accept a proposed alternative automatically.
  Recalculate check-in timing when the airline has already changed the schedule.
- Dangerous-goods and health declarations: report none without asking. Alejo has reviewed
  his travel kit for this standing answer.
- Passport or visa mismatch: never submit; email `ACTION REQUIRED`.
- Boarding-pass delivery: email PDF and wallet pass to `alejoacelas@gmail.com`.
- Failure alerts: email immediately, then retry only non-destructive technical failures.
- Destination arrival time: arrive before bedtime, normally 22:00 local. A somewhat later
  arrival is acceptable when it remains compatible with the timezone of the departure
  place.
- Departure-airport arrival lead time: [not yet specified; distinct from destination
  arrival time].
- Airport hotel: prefer under USD 200 per night and rank lower prices higher. Require a
  guest rating above 4/5; there is no hotel star-class requirement. Prefer a
  walk of at most 2 km from the airport; when an Uber is required, treat 5–20 minutes as
  equivalent rather than paying extra to save those minutes.
